What is PRINCE2 Agile?

PRINCE2 Agile is a project management methodology that combines the flexibility of Agile approaches with the governance and structure of PRINCE2. It helps organizations achieve agility without compromising project control, accountability, and business justification.

The framework provides guidance on how Agile concepts such as Scrum, Kanban, Lean, and iterative delivery can work within a PRINCE2 project environment. This combination enables organizations to respond quickly to changing business requirements while maintaining clear project oversight.

PRINCE2 Agile Certification Levels

PRINCE2 Agile Foundation

The Foundation certification introduces candidates to the core principles of PRINCE2 Agile. It focuses on terminology, concepts, governance structures, and Agile integration techniques.

Ideal for:

  • Project coordinators
  • Team members
  • Business analysts
  • Aspiring project managers
  • Agile practitioners

PRINCE2 Agile Practitioner

The Practitioner certification validates the ability to apply PRINCE2 Agile concepts in real-world project environments.

Ideal for:

  • Project managers
  • Program managers
  • Agile leaders
  • Consultants
  • PMO professionals

PRINCE2 Agile Certification Details

Foundation Exam

  • Question Type: Multiple Choice
  • Number of Questions: 50
  • Passing Score: Approximately 55%
  • Exam Duration: 60 Minutes
  • Closed Book Exam

Practitioner Exam

  • Scenario-Based Questions
  • Objective Testing Format
  • Passing Score: Approximately 60%
  • Exam Duration: Around 150 Minutes
  • Open Book Format

Candidates should always verify current exam requirements before scheduling their certification tests.
